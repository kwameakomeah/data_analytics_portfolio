# Customer Segmentation Analysis through RFM & Machine Learning

## Objective

Identify distinct customer segments based on purchasing behavior to enable targeted marketing, retention, and growth strategies.

---

## Dataset

The UCI Online Retail II dataset was used

---

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Machine Learning

---

## Process

1. Business problem
2. Data mining
3. Data cleaning
4. Exploratory analysis
5. RFM Feature engineering
6. Predictive analysis (KMeans clustering)
7. Insights and Visualization
8. Recommendations

---

## Results

- A strong Pareto effect emerged: small high-value customer base generating disproportionately high share of revenue, while over one-third of customers were inactive or at risk of churn.
- Customers followed predictable behavioral paths from new/low-value to potential loyalists, with identifiable intervention points to accelerate progression.
- Referral and email acquisition channels produced higher-value customers, while marketplaces performed better as discovery channels than retention drivers.
- Machine learning-based RFM customer segmentation proved a robust tool to improve retention, personalization, and revenue efficiency, transforming raw transaction data into a scalable decision support framework for senior marketing executives.
